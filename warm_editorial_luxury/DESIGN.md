## Brand & Style

This design system embodies warm, understated luxury and slow editorial poise. It draws inspiration from archival publishing, gallery monographs, and private atelier folios. Rejecting both the cold, blinding sterility of tech minimalism and the loud synthetic urgency of neon palettes, the visual tone is quiet, intellectual, and deliberate.

The emotional signature is calming, discerning, and tactile. Interface elements invite patient reading and considered contemplation. Surfaces feel like heavy uncoated vellum or fine cotton rag, balanced against deep, ink-rich typography that mirrors handset foundry metal types. Every layout is calibrated with generous spatial margins, restrained micro-interactions, and tailored proportion.

## Colors

The chromatic structure establishes an intimate, tactile environment through low-saturation mineral and organic pigments:

- **Canvas & Backgrounds:** Base canvas lives at warm champagne ivory (`#F9F8F5`), layered over elevated modules and panels set in alabaster parchment (`#F4F2EB`). Never introduce pure synthetic white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Typography & Dark Solids:** Deep espresso-noir (`#1A1917`) serves as the primary text, icon, and structural anchor color. Muted supporting copy steps down to weathered peat (`#524E48`), while subtle micro-captions settle into warm stone gray (`#827D74`).
- **Borders & Dividers:** Outlines rely on antique sand (`#DFDAD0`) and cashmere linen (`#ECE8E1`), functioning as feather-light physical seams rather than rigid wireframes.
- **Accents & States:** Refined antiqued bronze (`#A68248`) and brushed champagne gold (`#C5A059`) provide selective focal emphasis for active tab markers, badges, verified highlights, and fine focus indicators.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary elegance of **EB Garamond** with the clear geometric warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Headlines (EB Garamond):** Used exclusively for narrative lead-ins, editorial titles, quotes, and primary section headers. Letter spacing is subtly drawn in at larger sizes to mirror traditional book titling.
- **Body & Captions (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for effortless readability on digital screens, balancing geometric modernity with soft, humanist terminals.
- **Labels & Micro-indicators (Plus Jakarta Sans Uppercase or Small Caps):** Rendered with deliberate letter tracking (0.04em to 0.08em) at medium-to-semibold weights to ensure authoritative taxonomy without feeling stark or utilitarian.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an editorial broadsheet cadence using an asymmetrical 12-column grid on desktop, scaling to 6 columns on tablet, and collapsing to a 2-to-4 column modular flow on mobile devices.

- **Generous Canvas Margins:** Page gutters and margins prioritize unhurried negative space, allowing editorial imagery and long-form prose to breathe without visual congestion.
- **Reading Measure:** Text columns for continuous reading are strictly capped at 68 characters per line (approx. 640px) to uphold effortless optical scanning.
- **Spatial Alignment:** Internal card padding and element gaps utilize proportional increments of `space-sm`, `space-md`, and `space-lg`, ensuring layout rhythm matches the baseline grid of the typography.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through tactile surface tone transitions and subtle atmospheric micro-shadows rather than synthetic z-index stacking.

- **Surface Layering:** Depth is primarily established by transitioning from the main canvas (`#F9F8F5`) to raised cards and reading panels (`#F4F2EB`), edged with 1px hairline borders (`#DFDAD0`).
- **Warm Micro-Shadows:** Floating elements (dropdown menus, floating toolbars, elevated modals) use an ultra-diffused shadow tinted with deep umber: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(26, 25, 23, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(26, 25, 23, 0.03)`.
- **Low-Contrast Separation:** Avoid stark black-on-white elevation steps. Surfaces feel like physical paper resting quietly atop another sheet of linen.

## Shapes

The geometry of the interface avoids sharp industrial edges while consciously steering clear of exaggerated bubble-like curves. 

- **Primary Radius:** Core UI elements (buttons, form inputs, interactive tiles) maintain a refined 8px (`0.5rem`) corner radius.
- **Cards & Enclosures:** Larger structural surfaces, modals, and editorial visual frames implement a soft 12px to 16px radius (`rounded-lg`), imparting a tailor-made book-cloth finish.
- **Badges & Pills:** Discrete category stamps and audio playback controls use a full circular pill treatment when displaying isolated single-line metadata.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid espresso-noir (`#1A1917`) background with warm parchment (`#F9F8F5`) typography. Hover state settles into a softer charcoal (`#2D2B28`).
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Warm cashmere border (`#DFDAD0`) over transparent or `#F4F2EB` surface, text in `#1A1917`. On hover, the border darkens to muted bronze (`#A68248`).
- **Tertiary / Text Link:** Pure editorial text link in `#1A1917` with an offset 1px underline in `#A68248` positioned 4px beneath the baseline.

### Chips & Badges
- Built with a warm sand background (`#ECE8E1`), subtle hairline border (`#DFDAD0`), and uppercase `label-sm` typography tracking at `0.06em`. Used for genre tags, provenance markers, and collection indexes.

### Cards
- Surface: `#F4F2EB` with a continuous 1px outline in `#DFDAD0`.
- Padding: Generous `space-lg` internally.
- Imagery within cards carries a slight tonal dampening (1–2% warm tint) to seamlessly integrate photography with the parchment base.

### Form Inputs
- Background is set to translucent `#F9F8F5` bordered with `#DFDAD0`. 
- Active and focus states shift border color to antiqued bronze (`#A68248`) accompanied by a subtle 2px soft warm ring with zero harsh glare. Placeholder copy utilizes weathered stone (`#827D74`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square-rounded (checkbox) and concentric circle (radio) selectors with 1.5px outlines in `#524E48`. Selected states fill with `#1A1917` displaying an ivory icon check.

### Lists & Dividers
- Lists utilize generous vertical padding with delicate horizontal divider rules set at 1px using `#ECE8E1`. Optional ornamental section breaks feature a subtle centered bronze glyph or monogram marker.
